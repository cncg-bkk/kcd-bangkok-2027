## 1. About KCD Bangkok

Kubernetes Community Days (KCDs) are community-organized events, supported by the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF), that bring together adopters, contributors, and newcomers in the cloud native ecosystem.

KCD Bangkok 2027 is a one-day event for engineers, architects, SREs, platform teams, developers, and technology leaders across Thailand and Southeast Asia. Our goal is to **grow the local cloud native community**, share real-world lessons, and give **new voices a stage**.

We welcome speakers of all experience levels, and **first-time speakers are strongly encouraged to apply.**

## 3. Topics & Tracks

We are looking for practical, experience-driven talks. Suggested tracks:

### 🛠️ Track 1 — Platform Engineering & Developer Experience
- Internal developer platforms (IDP), golden paths, Backstage
- Multi-tenancy, self-service infrastructure
- GitOps (Argo CD, Flux), progressive delivery

### ☁️ Track 2 — Kubernetes Operations & Infrastructure
- Cluster lifecycle, upgrades, multi-cluster / hybrid / multi-cloud
- Networking (CNI, service mesh, Gateway API, eBPF)
- Storage, stateful workloads, backup & DR
- Cost optimization & FinOps

### 🔭 Track 3 — Observability & Reliability
- OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, Grafana stack (Loki, Tempo, Mimir)
- SRE practices, SLOs, incident response, chaos engineering

### 🔐 Track 4 — Security & Compliance
- Supply chain security (SBOM, Sigstore, SLSA)
- Policy as code (OPA/Gatekeeper, Kyverno), runtime security (Falco)
- Running cloud native in regulated industries (FinTech, banking, government)

### 🤖 Track 5 — AI/ML & Emerging Workloads on Kubernetes
- Model serving, GPU scheduling, AI platform engineering
- Data pipelines and streaming on Kubernetes
- WebAssembly, edge computing

### 🌱 Track 6 — Community, Culture & Career
- Contributing to CNCF projects, open source journeys
- Building and scaling platform teams
- Cloud native adoption stories from Thailand & SEA

> Don't see your topic? Submit anyway. Strong, relevant proposals outside these tracks are welcome.

## 4. Session Formats

| Format | Duration | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Talk** | 30 min (25 + 5 Q&A) | Standard technical session or case study |
| **Lightning Talk** | 5–10 min | One idea, one demo, one lesson. Great for first-timers |
| **Workshop** | 60–90 min | Hands-on lab; attendees bring laptops. *Limited slots* |
| **Panel** | 40 min | 3–4 panelists + moderator; submit with confirmed panelists |

## 5. Audience Level

Please tag your proposal with one level:

- **Beginner:** New to the topic; no prior knowledge assumed
- **Intermediate:** Familiar with basics; wants practical depth

## 7. What to Submit

Your submission on [Sessionize](https://sessionize.com/kcd-bangkok-2027/) should include:

1. **Session title**: clear and specific (max ~80 characters)
2. **Abstract**: what attendees will see on the agenda (max ~1,000 characters)
3. **Description for reviewers**: outline, key takeaways, demo plan, why you're the right speaker (not public)
4. **Track, format, level, and language**
5. **Speaker bio** (max ~500 characters) and **headshot**
6. **Past speaking experience / recordings** *(optional; not required)*
7. **Co-speakers** *(max 2 speakers per talk)*

---

## 8. Selection Criteria

Proposals are reviewed by a **community program committee** from multiple organizations. Each submission is scored on:

| Criteria | What we look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Relevance** | Fits cloud native / Kubernetes and our audience |
| **Originality** | Fresh insight, not a repeat of widely available content |
| **Practical value** | Real-world lessons, clear takeaways, reproducible demos |
| **Clarity** | Well-structured abstract with a clear narrative |
| **Vendor neutrality** | Educational, not a product pitch |
| **Diversity** | We actively seek diverse speakers, companies, and perspectives |

## 9. Speaker Guidelines

- ✅ **Vendor-neutral content.** Product pitches will not be accepted. Sponsor sessions are handled separately.
- ✅ The **[CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md)** applies to all speakers and content.
- ✅ **Recording consent.** Sessions may be recorded and published. Let us know if you have concerns.
- ✅ **One primary submission per speaker is recommended** (max 2).
- ✅ **AI-assisted proposals are fine**, but the content and experience must be your own.
- ❌ Do not submit talks you cannot personally deliver on the event date.

## 12. Tips for a Strong Proposal

1. **Lead with the problem.** What pain did you solve?
2. **Be specific.** "How we cut cluster costs by 40% with Karpenter" beats "Kubernetes cost optimization."
3. **State the takeaways.** What will attendees be able to do after your talk?
4. **Show, don't sell.** Share architecture, numbers, failures, and lessons learned.
5. **Proofread.** Reviewers notice.
